Data capture and privacy agreement

Before WorkTrace records your screen or browser actions, review what we capture and how it is handled.

  • We capture clicks, navigation, form interactions, and screenshots during a session.
  • Passwords are never recorded. Other values are masked before storage when possible.
  • Captured data stays within your tenant workspace to generate editable SOP drafts.

Recording stays disabled without consent. You can review this agreement again before starting a session.
